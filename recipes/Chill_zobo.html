Chill Zobo

Ingredients:

For about 2 to 3 litres:

  1. 🌺 2 cups dried zobo leaves (dried hibiscus petals)
  2. 💧 2.5–3 litres water
  3. 🍍 1 medium pineapple — preferably fresh
  4. 🍍 Pineapple peel — washed thoroughly
  5. 🫚 1 large piece of ginger
  6. 🍋 1–2 lemons or limes (optional)
  7. 🍬 Sugar — to taste
  8. 🌿 Cloves (kanumfari) — about 1–2 teaspoons (optional, but gives it a great flavour)

Procedure:

  1. Wash the zobo leaves Rinse the dried zobo leaves thoroughly with clean water to remove dust and dirt.
  2. Prepare the pineapple and ginger Wash the pineapple, peel it, and cut the fruit into pieces. Wash and peel the ginger, then blend or crush it.
  3. Boil the zobo leaves Put the washed zobo leaves into a large pot and add about 2.5–3 litres of water.
  4. Add the pineapple peel and ginger Add the pineapple peel and crushed/blended ginger to the pot.
  5. Strain the drink Remove the pot from the heat and allow it to cool slightly. Strain the mixture through a clean sieve to remove the leaves, ginger and pineapple pieces.
  6. Sweeten it Add sugar to taste. You can also add a little pineapple juice or lemon/lime juice for extra flavour.
  7. Cool the zobo Allow it to cool completely, then refrigerate it until cold.
  8. Serve 🧊 Serve chilled, optionally with ice cubes, pineapple pieces, or mint leaves.

    9. 🔥 Tip: Don't add too much sugar at once. Add a little, taste, and gradually adjust until you get the sweetness you want.
