Egusi soup

Ingredients:

  1. 2 cups ground egusi (melon seeds)
  2. 1 kg assorted meat (beef, goat meat, shaki, etc.)
  3. 500 g stockfish (optional)
  4. 200–300 g dried or smoked fish
  5. 1–2 cups palm oil
  6. 2–3 red bell peppers
  7. 1–2 scotch bonnet peppers (ata rodo)
  8. 1 large onion
  9. 2–3 tablespoons ground crayfish
  10. 2–3 seasoning cubes
  11. Salt to taste
  12. 4–6 cups meat stock or water
    13. Vegetable leaves such as:
    1. Ugu (pumpkin leaves), or
    2. Spinach
    3. Bitter leaf (optional)
  14. 1–2 tablespoons ground pepper (optional, depending on how spicy you want it)

Optional additions

  1. Ponmo (cow skin)
  2. Periwinkle
  3. Extra smoked fish
  4. Ogiri for a stronger traditional flavour

Procedures:

  1. Prepare the meat
    1. Wash the meat and put it in a pot.
    2. Add chopped onion, seasoning cube and salt.
    3. Add a little water and cook until the meat is tender.
    4. Keep the meat stock.
  2. Prepare the egusi
    1. Grind the egusi until it becomes a fine powder
    2. You can mix it with a little water to form a thick paste, or add it directly later.
  3. Heat the palm oil
    1. Pour palm oil into a clean pot.
    2. Heat it gently—don't let it smoke excessively.
    3. Add some chopped onion and fry briefly.
  4. Add the pepper
    1. Add your blended pepper and stir.
    2. Allow it to cook for about 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Add the egusi
    1. Add the ground egusi gradually while stirring.
    2. Cook for about 5–10 minutes.
    3. If it becomes too thick, add a little meat stock.
  6. Add the meat and stock
    1. Add the cooked meat, stockfish/cow skin and crayfish if using.
    2. Add enough stock/water to get your preferred thickness.
    3. Stir well and allow it to simmer for 10–15 minutes.
  7. Add the vegetables
    1. Wash and chop the ugu or spinach.
    2. Add it to the soup and stir.
    3. Cook for another 3–5 minutes so the vegetables remain fresh.
  8. Taste and adjust
    1. Taste the soup and add salt or seasoning if necessary.
    2. Let it simmer for another 2–3 minutes, then turn off the heat.

    You can serve with Pounded yam, eba, fufu, semovita, amala, or rice.
