Smokey Jollof

Ingredients:

Serves about 5 to 6 people

  1. 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  2. 5–6 large tomatoes
  3. 2 red bell peppers (Tatashe)
  4. 2–3 Scotch bonnet peppers (Ata Rodo) — adjust to taste
  5. 2 large onions
  6. 4 cloves garlic
  7. 1 small piece ginger
  8. ½ cup vegetable oil
  9. 2–3 tablespoons tomato paste
  10. 3–4 cups chicken stock or water
  11. 2–3 seasoning cubes
  12. 1 teaspoon curry powder
  13. 1 teaspoon thyme
  14. 1 teaspoon white or black pepper
  15. Salt to taste
  16. 2 bay leaves (optional)
  17. 1 teaspoon smoked paprika (optional, for extra smokiness)
  18. Cooked chicken or beef (optional)

Procedure:

  1. Wash and prepare the ingredients Wash the tomatoes, peppers, onions, ginger and garlic. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, Scotch bonnet peppers, ginger, garlic and one onion until smooth.
  2. Parboil the rice Rinse the rice thoroughly. Parboil it for about 5–7 minutes, then drain and set aside. Avoid overcooking it.
  3. Prepare the stew base Heat vegetable oil in a large pot. Add chopped onions and fry for about 2–3 minutes.
  4. Fry the tomato paste Add the tomato paste and fry for about 3–5 minutes, stirring continuously. This helps remove the raw tomato taste.
  5. Add the blended pepper mixture Pour in the blended tomato and pepper mixture. Stir well and allow it to cook for about 15–20 minutes, until the mixture thickens and the excess water reduces.
  6. Season the stew Add curry powder, thyme, seasoning cubes, bay leaves, pepper and salt. Stir everything together.
  7. Add the stock Pour in your chicken stock or water and bring the mixture to a boil. Taste the sauce and adjust the seasoning if necessary.
  8. Add the rice Add the parboiled rice and stir well so that the rice is evenly coated with the tomato mixture.
  9. Cover and cook Cover the pot tightly with foil or a well-fitting lid. Reduce the heat to low and allow the rice to cook for about 20–30 minutes.
  10. Create the smoky flavour 🔥 When the rice is almost completely cooked, reduce the heat even further and allow it to stay on the bottom of the pot for a few minutes until you hear a gentle sizzling sound. This creates the slightly toasted layer responsible for the smoky party-Jollof flavour. Don't let it burn excessively.
  11. Mix gently Turn off the heat and allow the rice to rest for about 5 minutes. Remove the bay leaves, then gently stir the rice so the smoky bottom layer mixes through.
  12. Serve 🍗 Serve hot with fried or grilled chicken, fried plantain, coleslaw, or your preferred side.

Key secret: Don't constantly stir the rice while it's cooking. Keeping the pot covered and cooking slowly helps the rice absorb the stew and develop that delicious smoky flavour.
